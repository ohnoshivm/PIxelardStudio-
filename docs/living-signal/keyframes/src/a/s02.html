Type and colour: options, not decisions.
Two type directions and three palette directions, all obeying the same rules. Interim open-source faces stand in for a licensed or custom "Pixelards Setting" family. Nothing here is final.

Direction 1 · Wide-range grotesk

Fit by the width axis (wdth 50–150). Interim: Anybody. Licensed candidates: GT Flexa, Obviously. Long term: a custom family with wdth 50–170.

Direction 2 · Optical-size serif

Fit by size, optical size and phrase breaks, the way a newspaper re-sets a story. Interim: Newsreader (opsz 6–72). Licensed candidates: Tiempos, GT Super.
